## Brand & Style
The brand embodies an understated, botanical luxury tailored for the discerning Pakistani consumer seeking clean, clinically validated organic skincare. The visual narrative balances earthy purity with sophisticated apothecary discipline.

### Personality & Emotional Resonance
- **Tactile Calm:** Evoking the serenity of morning dew on herbal foliage and smooth travertine stone.
- **Rooted Authenticity:** Grounded, conscious formulations honoring indigenous botanical heritage through a modern high-end lens.
- **Purity & Trust:** Reassurance through generous negative space, deliberate typographic pacing, and whisper-quiet organic neutrals rather than clinical sterility.

### Aesthetic Movement
**Minimalist Organic Luxury:** High-contrast editorial typography paired with ultra-clean surfaces, micro-borders, and tactile pill geometry. Interfaces rely on breathing room, soft tonal hierarchy, and rich botanical color blocking to create an aspirational, boutique shopping experience.

## Colors
The palette draws directly from raw travertine stone, cured botanical resins, and crushed sage foliage. 

- **Primary (`#2D3830` - Dark Forest):** Used for primary conversion touchpoints, high-emphasis action triggers, header bars, and primary badges. Conveys deep botanical authority and grounding.
- **Secondary (`#8FA391` - Herbarium Sage):** Accents, active category filters, active indicators, and secondary illustrative borders.
- **Tertiary (`#DCE4DC` - Soft Sage Tint):** Subtle structural fills, active chip backgrounds, badge backdrops, and promotional micro-surfaces.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F6F3EC` - Travertine Cream):** The global viewport background, offering an organic warmth superior to cold pure white.
- **Surface Pure (`#FFFFFF`):** High-layer elevation for product cards, floating modals, slide-overs, and bottom sheets to ensure clear item separation against travertine backgrounds.
- **Text Ink (`#232924` - Charcoal Ink):** Primary editorial copy, titles, and high-readability price displays.
- **Text Muted (`#5C665E` - Muted Slate):** Ingredient percentages, PKR tax notations, metadata, and placeholder copy.

## Typography
Typographic rhythm relies on an intentional juxtaposition between classical editorial romance and precise, humanist geometry.

- **Editorial Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Renders high-tier collection titles, storytelling narratives, and hero banners. Provides an architectural, high-couture aesthetic.
- **Accents & By-lines (`subheading-editorial`):** Styled strictly in italics with delicate tracking for brand sign-offs (e.g., *"By Robaha"*), origin certificates, and botanical extraction methods.
- **Interface & Commerce Engine (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Clean, open letterforms engineered for quick scanning across product specifications, PKR pricing breakdowns, and checkout sequences.
- **Pakistani Currency Conventions:** Prices must always prefix the regional code (e.g., `PKR 4,850`) using `label-lg` or `price-headline` with zero decimal places for whole rupees.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout architecture enforces expansive luxury spacing to prevent visual clutter and accentuate product packaging.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column responsive grid with `margin: 3rem` (48px) and `gutter: 1.5rem` (24px). Maximum container width is constrained to `1320px` to maintain comfortable eye-tracking distance.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column responsive grid with `margin: 2rem` (32px) and `gutter: 1.25rem` (20px).
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column responsive grid with `margin-mobile: 1.25rem` (20px) and `gutter-mobile: 1rem` (16px).

### Section Rhythms
- Product detail pages and long-form narrative modules separate content blocks using `space-xl` (48px) on mobile and double `space-xl` (96px) on desktop to mirror high-fashion printed publications.
- Form inputs, card clusters, and filter carousels maintain compact rhythmic flow via `space-md` and `space-sm`.

## Elevation & Depth
In alignment with the clean organic philosophy, drop shadows are heavily restricted in favor of structural layer boundaries and translucent glass treatments.

- **Primary Surfaces:** Pure white `#FFFFFF` cards sit directly over the `#F6F3EC` travertine canvas, creating depth purely through tone and value separation.
- **Tonal Borders:** When needed, cards, input frames, and dialog dividers employ a subtle 1px border using `#8FA391` at 20% opacity (`rgba(143, 163, 145, 0.20)`).
- **Floating Modals & Drawers:** Filter panels, cart slide-outs, and quick-add modals use a deep botanical ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 16px 40px -12px rgba(45, 56, 48, 0.08)`.
- **Botanical Blur Layer:** Sticky navigation bars and floating mobile action anchors use a frosted travertine glass backdrop: `background: rgba(246, 243, 236, 0.85); backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.

## Shapes
The shape philosophy merges geometric softness with ergonomic ease. 

- **Standard Containers & Cards:** Set to `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) to gently frame product imagery, clinical review cards, and promotional callouts without looking childish.
- **Interactive Action Items:** Primary buttons, search inputs, active filter badges, and toast notifications feature a complete `rounded-full` (9999px) pill shape, reinforcing the smooth pebble and water-droplet identity of clean skincare.
- **Thumbnails & Media Previews:** Medium roundedness (0.75rem / 12px) to maintain balance alongside the exterior card curves.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Add to Cart / Checkout):** Background `#2D3830` with `#FFFFFF` text. Fully pill-shaped (`rounded-full`). Padding: `14px 28px`. Hover: Background transitions to `#3D4C41` with smooth 200ms ease. Focus: 2px offset outline in `#8FA391`.
- **Secondary Action (Discover / Learn More):** Outlined pill with 1.5px border in `#2D3830`, transparent background, and `#232924` text. Hover fills to `#DCE4DC` with zero border jump.
- **Text & Link Actions:** Plain `#232924` text with an animated 1px underline in `#8FA391`, floating 4px beneath the baseline.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status & Organic Claims ("100% Halal", "Wildcrafted Bakuchiol"):** Background `#DCE4DC`, text `#2D3830`, font `label-sm`, rounded-full pill shape. Vertical padding: 4px; horizontal padding: 12px.
- **Filter Tags:** Inactive filters use `#FFFFFF` background with 1px border of `rgba(143, 163, 145, 0.3)`. Active filters use `#2D3830` background with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Product Cards
- **Structure:** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface, `rounded-xl` shape, subtle 1px border in `rgba(143, 163, 145, 0.15)`.
- **Image Container:** Aspect ratio 4:5 with an integrated soft neutral background (`#F0EDE6`) and smooth inset radii.
- **Content Hierarchy:** Category tag in uppercase `label-sm` (`#5C665E`), product title in `headline-sm` (`#232924`), extraction detail in `subheading-editorial` (`#8FA391`), and bold price format in `price-headline` (e.g., `PKR 6,400`).

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Minimum height 48px, background `#FFFFFF`, rounded-full pill contour. Border: 1px solid `rgba(143, 163, 145, 0.35)`. Internal padding: `12px 20px`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts to solid `#8FA391` with zero fuzzy focus rings, preserving minimal editorial discipline.
- **Labels:** Positioned outside the input field in `label-md` tracking using `#232924`.

### Selection Controls
- **Radio Buttons & Checkboxes:** Circular elements with 20px diameter. Checked state utilizes a solid `#2D3830` fill with a pure white micro-checkmark or center pip. Unchecked states feature an unadorned 1.5px ring in `#8FA391`.

### Specialized Commerce Components
- **PKR Currency Display:** Uniform treatment with the currency designation capitalized in `label-sm` (`PKR`) accompanied by tabular numbers for rapid price comparison across the catalog.
- **Ingredient Accordion:** Edge-to-edge list item separated by 1px rules in `#DCE4DC`. Expanded state reveals an organic cream inset container (`#FBF9F6`) detailing botanical origins and safe synthetic clarifications.
